FAQ

What does "agent-ready" mean?

An agent-ready business is one an AI assistant (like ChatGPT, Google's AI Mode or Gemini) can find, understand, book and pay without a person having to call around. That means accurate listings, clear services and prices, online booking with real availability, and online payment.

Do I need new software?

Usually not. Most of the work is fixing and connecting what you already have: your Google Business Profile, your website, and your booking and payment tools. If you don't have online booking or payments, we recommend the simplest option that fits your business.

Can you guarantee AI tools will recommend my business?

No. No one can. AI companies don't publish exactly how they choose businesses. What we can do is remove the reasons an AI tool would skip you: missing or wrong information, unclear services and prices, and no way to book or pay.
